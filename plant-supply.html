Our Approach

Right Plant. Right Place.
Right Performance.

At CHF, plant supply is more than procurement. Every plant is selected with careful consideration of climate, site conditions, design objectives and long-term performance.

Our extensive network of trusted growers and specialist nurseries across India allows us to source healthy, resilient and visually impactful plant material for projects of every scale.
